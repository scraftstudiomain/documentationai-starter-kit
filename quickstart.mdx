<Callout kind="info" title="Prerequisites">
Before you begin, ensure you have:
- A modern web browser like Chrome, Firefox, or Safari
- An email address for account verification
- Optional: A GitHub account for content import
</Callout>

## Sign Up and Log In

Create your Scraft account to access the documentation platform.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit Scraft Dashboard" icon="monitor">
    Open your browser and navigate to `https://app.scraft.com`.

    Click **Sign Up** in the top right corner.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Register Account" icon="user-plus">
    Enter your email, create a password, and provide your name.

    Verify your email by clicking the link sent to your inbox.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Log In" icon="log-in">
    Return to `https://app.scraft.com` and sign in with your credentials.

    You'll land on the main dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Create Your First Documentation Space

Set up a new project to organize your docs.

<Steps>
  <Step title="New Space" icon="plus">
    From the dashboard, click **New Documentation Space**.

    Enter a name like `My Project Docs` and select a starter template.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure Basics" icon="settings">
    Choose your brand color (e.g., `#EE964B`) and add a description.

    Click **Create** to generate the space.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Import Initial Content

Populate your space quickly with existing content.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Manual Upload" icon="upload">
    Drag and drop MDX files into the **Import** section.

    <CodeGroup tabs="MDX Example">
    ````mdx
    ## Welcome

    This is your first page.
    ````

    ````mdx
    <Callout kind="success">Import complete!</Callout>
    ````
    </CodeGroup>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="GitHub Repo" icon="github">
    Connect your GitHub account via **Integrations > GitHub**.

    Select a repo and import all markdown/MDX files automatically.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Start with 3-5 pages for a minimal viable docs site. Use the search bar to navigate.
</Callout>
