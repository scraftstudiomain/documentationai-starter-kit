---
title: Document Not Saving
description: Fix saving issues in Scraft.
---

## Symptoms

- Edits do not appear after refreshing
- "Failed to save" error on top bar

## Solutions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your internet connection">
    ````bash
    ping scraft.io
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Retry saving">
    Click the **Save** button again or refresh the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear browser cache">
    Try clearing cache if issues persist.
  </Step>
</Steps>
