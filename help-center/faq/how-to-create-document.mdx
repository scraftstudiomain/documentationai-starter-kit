---
title: How do I create a new document?
description: Quick steps to start a document in Scraft.
---

## Steps

1. Click the **New Document** button in the Scraft dashboard.
2. Enter the document title and description.
3. Choose or create a folder if needed.
4. Click **Create** to open the editor.
5. Start adding your documentation content.

## Tip

You can move documents between folders anytime from the sidebar menu.
