---
title: Organizing Projects
description: How to organize projects using folders in Scraft.
---

## Overview

Group related documents into folders for better management.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a folder">
    1. In the sidebar, click **New Folder**.
    2. Name your folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move documents">
    1. Drag and drop documents into your folder.
    2. Or use the document's menu: **Move to...**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access organized docs">
    Click a folder to view all documents inside.
  </Step>
</Steps>
